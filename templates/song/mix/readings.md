# <Song title> — spectrograph readings

One entry per capture in this folder. **A PNG with no entry here is not a measurement**,
because nobody can reproduce it later.

Every entry pins the five variables that make two captures comparable. Two screenshots at
different loop points, analyzer settings or master levels tell you nothing.

---
